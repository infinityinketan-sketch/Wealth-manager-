Privacy Policy

Wealth Manager for Android · Effective 25 October 2026
In short: your SMS, transactions, amounts, balances and bank details stay on your phone and are never sent to any server. To run your account and free trial you sign in with Google, and we keep your name, email and a few app details listed below. We do not sell or share your data, and you can delete your account at any time.

Who we are

Wealth Manager is published by DEVELOPER_NAME, Pune, India ("we"). For India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, we are the Data Fiduciary for the account data described below. Contact and grievances: SUPPORT_EMAIL.

Data that stays only on your phone

  • SMS messages (only with your permission). The app finds messages from banks, card issuers, UPI apps and lenders and turns them into transactions (amount, date, last digits of the account, merchant, balance). Personal messages are skipped and not stored.
  • Everything you enter or the app works out: transactions, categories, budgets, reminders, notes, balances.

None of this is uploaded, synced or shared. It is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Android cloud backup is turned off for this app.

Consent and your choices

  • Before sign-in the app shows what is kept and asks for your consent. Feature-use sharing is a separate, optional choice.
  • You can turn feature-use sharing off at any time in More → Account; the counts already stored are deleted.
  • You can withdraw consent for your account data by deleting your account (below). The app cannot be used without an account.

Where it is stored and who processes it

Account data is stored in Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), operated by Google as our processor, and is encrypted in transit and at rest. Each person can only read their own record. Sign-in is handled by Google under Google's Privacy Policy.

Purchases

Subscriptions (monthly, yearly), passes (1 month, 1 year) and the lifetime unlock are sold and processed by Google Play, including renewals and cancellation. We never see your card, UPI or bank details; the app only learns from Google Play which plan is active, and we store that plan name with your account.

How long we keep it

  • Feedback: until it is dealt with, at most 2 years; it is also deleted with your account.
  • Account data: while your account exists. When you delete your account it is erased straight away from our database (and within 30 days from any backups).
  • After deletion we keep only a one-way code (a SHA-256 hash) of your email and the trial start date, so the free trial cannot be claimed again. It cannot be turned back into your email.
  • Accounts not used for 3 years may be deleted.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or erase your data, withdraw consent, raise a grievance, or (under the DPDP Act) nominate someone to act for you, by writing to SUPPORT_EMAIL. We reply within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Deleting your account

In the app: More → Account → Delete account. Without the app: see how to request deletion. Uninstalling the app removes the data on your phone; it does not delete your account.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your personal data with anyone, and there are no ads. We would disclose data only if required by Indian law.

Age

Wealth Manager is for people aged 18 and over.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and its date, and show a notice in the app for important changes.
