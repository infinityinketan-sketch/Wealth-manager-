Privacy Policy

Wealth Manager for Android · Effective 24 October 2026
In short: Wealth Manager reads bank and payment SMS on your phone to track your money. Everything stays on your phone. The app has no internet permission, has no account or login, and sends no data to us or to anyone else.

What the app reads

  • SMS messages (only with your permission). The app looks for messages from banks, card issuers, UPI apps and lenders, and turns them into transactions: amount, date, account ending digits, merchant name and balance where the bank includes it. Personal messages are skipped and not stored.
  • Entries you type in: cash spending, categories, budgets, reminders and notes.

Where your data is kept

  • Only in the app's private storage on your phone. Other apps cannot read it.
  • The app is built without the Android INTERNET permission, so it cannot upload anything, even by mistake.
  • Android's automatic cloud backup is turned off for this app, so your data is not copied to Google Drive behind your back.
  • We run no servers and use no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting services. If the app crashes, the details are saved on your phone and you can choose to copy them yourself (long numbers are masked).

Backup files

If you choose "Back up now", the app writes one file to the place you pick (for example your Downloads folder or a Drive folder you select). The file is encrypted with AES-256 using a password only you know; we cannot open it and cannot recover a forgotten password. Where you keep that file is up to you.

Purchases

Wealth Manager Pro is a one-time in-app purchase handled entirely by Google Play. Google Play processes your payment under Google's Privacy Policy. The app only receives a yes/no answer from the Google Play app on your phone about whether Pro was bought. We never see your card, UPI or payment details.

Notifications

With your permission, the app shows notifications on your phone (for example "what was this payment for?" and bill due reminders). They are created on the phone; nothing is sent from a server.

Sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer your data, because we never receive it.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android Settings → Apps → Wealth Manager → Storage. This removes everything. Backup files you saved yourself are not touched; delete them where you stored them.

Children

The app is meant for adults managing their own money and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
