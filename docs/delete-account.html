Delete your account

Wealth Manager for Android

In the app (quickest)

Open Wealth Manager → More → Account → Delete account. If your free trial has ended, the button is on the unlock screen.

Without the app

Email SUPPORT_EMAIL from the Google account you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my Wealth Manager account". We delete it within 7 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your name, email, sign-up and last-open dates, app/Android version, phone model, Pro status and feature-use counts.
  • Your sign-in account in our system.

What is kept

  • Only a one-way code (hash) of your email with your trial start date, so the free trial is not given twice. It cannot be turned back into your email.
  • Your Pro purchase stays with your Google Play account (that is Google's record, not ours).
  • Data on your phone (transactions etc.) is never on our server; uninstall the app to remove it.
